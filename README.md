# Generative AI (GenAI)

This repository contains my hands-on assignments, practical implementations, and experiments as part of my learning journey in **Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI)**.

The objective is to gain practical experience by implementing concepts, exploring AI tools and frameworks, and understanding how different components of Generative AI work.

- Understand the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI.
- Explore neural networks and their fundamental building blocks.
- Learn about different activation functions and their applications.
- Explore Hugging Face models and libraries.
- Develop practical skills through coding assignments and experiments.
- Build a strong foundation in Generative AI technologies.
